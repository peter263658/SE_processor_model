============================================================
Layer Configuration: Layer2_16to32
============================================================

Number Format:
  Type: IEEE 754 Half Precision (FP16)
  Total bits: 16
  WXP (Exponent): 5 bits
  FRAC (Fraction): 10 bits
  Exponent bias: 15
  Bit layout: [sign(1)][exp(5)][frac(10)]

Architecture:
  Type: Shared Weights (左右耳共用)
  Description: 左右聲道使用相同的 Encoder/Decoder 權重

Layer Dimensions:
  in_channels:  16 (complex)
  out_channels: 32 (complex)
  freq_in:      16
  freq_out:     8

Convolution Parameters:
  kernel_size: (5, 1)
  stride:      (2, 1)
  padding:     (2, 0)

Data Files:
  input.dat:
    - Shape: [16, 16]
    - Size: 256 complex numbers
    - Description: 輸入特徵 (單聲道等效)
    - Format: [imag_fp16][real_fp16] per line (32-bit)

  dw_weight.dat:
    - Shape: [16, 5]
    - Size: 80 complex numbers
    - Description: Depthwise 卷積權重 (左右耳共用)
    - Format: [imag_fp16][real_fp16] per line (32-bit)

  pw_weight.dat:
    - Shape: [32, 16]
    - Size: 512 complex numbers
    - Description: Pointwise 卷積權重 (左右耳共用)
    - Format: [imag_fp16][real_fp16] per line (32-bit)

  dw_output.dat:
    - Shape: [16, 8]
    - Size: 128 complex numbers
    - Description: Depthwise 輸出 (中間驗證用)
    - Format: [imag_fp16][real_fp16] per line (32-bit)

  final_output.dat:
    - Shape: [32, 8]
    - Size: 256 complex numbers
    - Description: 最終輸出 (Golden reference)
    - Format: [imag_fp16][real_fp16] per line (32-bit)

Computational Complexity (per time frame, 單聲道):
  Depthwise:
    - Complex MACs: 640
    - Real MACs:    2560
  Pointwise:
    - Complex MACs: 4096
    - Real MACs:    16384
  Total:
    - Complex MACs: 4736
    - Real MACs:    18944
    - Cycles @ 256 Real MACs: 74

Binaural Processing:
  - 左右耳使用相同權重
  - 可循序處理 (L→R) 或並行處理
  - 循序處理: 74 cycles/channel
  - 並行處理: 74 cycles/frame (需2組PEG)
