Hardware Configuration for Binaural Conv Encoder (IEEE FP16)
================================================================================

數值格式:
  Format: IEEE 754 Half Precision (FP16)
  Total bits: 16
  WXP (Exponent): 5 bits (bias=15)
  FRAC (Fraction): 10 bits
  Bit layout: [sign(1)][exp(5)][frac(10)]
  Range: ~6e-5 to ~6.5e4
  Precision: ~3 decimal digits

架構特性:
  - 左右耳共用同一組 Encoder/Decoder 權重
  - STFT Output: 64 complex frequency bins
  - Kernel Size: (5, 1) - 頻率維度卷積
  - Stride: (2, 1), Padding: (2, 0)
  - Total MACs: 256 Real = 64 Complex

處理策略:
  Option 1: 循序處理 (Sequential)
    - 先處理左耳，再處理右耳
    - 總延遲: ~1585 cycles/frame
    - 硬體需求: 1 組 PEG (256 MACs)
    - 優點: 最小面積

  Option 2: 並行處理 (Parallel)
    - 同時處理左右耳
    - 總延遲: ~792 cycles/frame
    - 硬體需求: 2 組 PEG (512 MACs)
    - 優點: 最低延遲

推薦配置 (循序處理):
  MAC Units: 256 Real MACs = 64 Complex MACs (FP16)
  SRAM: 8 Banks × 32-bit × 20 depth
    - Total: 5,120 bits (160 complex weights)
    - Bandwidth: 256 bits/cycle
    - Format: [imag_fp16(16)][real_fp16(16)]

Weight 存取策略:
  共用權重:
    - Depthwise: 5 weights per channel
    - Pointwise: varies by layer
    - 左右耳重用同一組 SRAM weights

  Bank Interleaving:
    Depthwise: W[0]→Bank0, W[1]→Bank1, ..., W[4]→Bank4
    Pointwise: 按 output channel 分組

性能估算 (@ 16kHz, hop=80 samples):
  Frame interval: 5ms = 80,000 cycles @ 16MHz
  Processing time: 1585 cycles (循序)
  Utilization: 1.98%
  結論: 256 MACs 綽綽有餘

FP16 vs BFloat16 比較:
  FP16 優勢:
    - 更高精度 (10-bit frac vs 7-bit frac)
    - 更適合音訊處理的動態範圍
    - IEEE 標準，工具鏈支援完整
  BFloat16 優勢:
    - 更大數值範圍 (8-bit exp vs 5-bit exp)
    - 與 FP32 轉換簡單 (直接截斷)
